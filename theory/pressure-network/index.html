Wind tunnel model

A one-dimensional flow model is developed to predict the flow rates in the various sections of the wind tunnel. In this one-dimensional model, the flow is characterized by the volumetric flow rates, QiQ_iQi​, in each section and the static pressure at each section interface, pip_ipi​ (red nodes). Pressures and velocities represent spatial means over the cross-section area. Non-uniformities such as boundary layers, velocity deficits, and secondary flows are not resolved individually (Barlow et al., 1999).

Pressure equations​

The centrifugal fan is the only active element in the circuit. Its rotational speed nnn is the primary control variable for setting the wind-tunnel operating condition. At a given speed the fan delivers a pressure rise, Δpfan≡p7−p6\Delta p_\text{fan} \equiv p_7 - p_6Δpfan​≡p7​−p6​, that depends on the volume flow, Qfan≡Q6Q_\text{fan} \equiv Q_6Qfan​≡Q6​, through its characteristic curve

Δpfan=Δpfan(Qfan,n).\Delta p_\text{fan} = \Delta p_\text{fan}(Q_\text{fan}, n).Δpfan​=Δpfan​(Qfan​,n).

For all other sections (i≠6i \neq 6i=6), Bernoulli's equation with an irreversible loss gives

pi+1−pi=qi−qi+1−Ki qi,p_{i+1} - p_{i} = q_i - q_{i+1} - K_i\,q_i,pi+1​−pi​=qi​−qi+1​−Ki​qi​,

where Ki≥0K_i \geq 0Ki​≥0 is the dimensionless loss coefficient of section iii (Barlow et al., 1999).

Summing the pressure equations around the closed loop, the circuit balance equation is obtained as

Δpfan(Qfan,n)=∑i≠6Ki qi,\Delta p_\text{fan}(Q_\text{fan},n) = \sum_{i \neq 6} K_i\,q_i,Δpfan​(Qfan​,n)=i=6∑​Ki​qi​,

where it is assumed that the dynamic pressure does not change over the fan (q6=q7q_6 = q_7q6​=q7​). This assumption is justified by the practically identical inflow and outflow areas of this section, as well as its negligible leakage. The circuit balance conveys that the intersection of the fan characteristic with the circuit resistance curve determines the operating flow rate QfanQ_\text{fan}Qfan​ for a given fan speed nnn.

Mass-balance equations​

Although PROTOFLOW is a nearly closed circuit, imperfections introduce small leaks between the tunnel interior and the surrounding atmosphere. Even though the resulting mass flows are small compared to the main circulation, they have a disproportionate pressure effect. In a perfectly sealed circuit, the absolute pressure distribution would be entirely indeterminate, whereas even minor leakage anchors the absolute level.

For each passive section the leakage is modeled as

Qi+1−QiQi=Λi patm−piqii≠6,\frac{Q_{i+1} - Q_i}{Q_i} = \Lambda_i\,\frac{p_\text{atm} - p_i}{q_i} \qquad i \neq 6,Qi​Qi+1​−Qi​​=Λi​qi​patm​−pi​​i=6,

where Λi\Lambda_iΛi​ is the dimensionless leakage coefficient. The right-hand side is positive (air enters) when pi<patmp_i < p_\text{atm}pi​<patm​ and negative (air escapes) when pi>patmp_i > p_\text{atm}pi​>patm​.

Summing all the leakage equations over the closed circuit gives the global mass balance

∑i=112Λi Qi patm−piqi=0,\sum_{i=1}^{12} \Lambda_i\,Q_i\,\frac{p_\text{atm} - p_i}{q_i} = 0,i=1∑12​Λi​Qi​qi​patm​−pi​​=0,

which pins the absolute pressure level of the circuit.

Settling chamber​

Solution algorithm​

The pressures and flow rates are determined by a fixed-point iteration. Each iteration consists of three steps.

Step 1 — Flow-rate distribution. Treat the gauge pressures Δpi=patm−pi\Delta p_i = p_\text{atm} - p_iΔpi​=patm​−pi​ as known, assuming Δpi=0\Delta p_i = 0Δpi​=0 for all iii for the first iteration. The flow rates can then be expressed as a function of QfanQ_\text{fan}Qfan​:

Qi={Qfan+∑j=712ΛjQjΔpjqj+∑j=1i−1ΛjQjΔpjqji=1,…,5Qfani=6,7Qfan+∑j=7i−1ΛjQjΔpjqji=8,…,12Q_i = \begin{cases} Q_\text{fan} + \sum_{j=7}^{12} \Lambda_j Q_j \frac{\Delta p_j}{q_j} + \sum_{j=1}^{i-1} \Lambda_j Q_j \frac{\Delta p_j}{q_j} & i = 1, \ldots, 5\\ Q_\text{fan} & i=6,7 \\ Q_\text{fan} + \sum_{j=7}^{i-1} \Lambda_j Q_j \frac{\Delta p_j}{q_j} & i = 8, \ldots, 12 \end{cases}Qi​=⎩⎨⎧​Qfan​+∑j=712​Λj​Qj​qj​Δpj​​+∑j=1i−1​Λj​Qj​qj​Δpj​​Qfan​Qfan​+∑j=7i−1​Λj​Qj​qj​Δpj​​​i=1,…,5i=6,7i=8,…,12​

Step 2 — Fan operating point. With all QiQ_iQi​ expressed in terms of QfanQ_\text{fan}Qfan​, the circuit pressure balance

Δpfan(Qfan,n)=∑i≠6Ki qi(Qfan)\Delta p_\text{fan}(Q_\text{fan}, n) = \sum_{i \neq 6} K_i\,q_i(Q_\text{fan})Δpfan​(Qfan​,n)=i=6∑​Ki​qi​(Qfan​)
